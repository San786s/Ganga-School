About Us

Welcome to Ganga Secondary School

At Ganga Secondary School (formerly DPS School), we provide a deeply rooted, community-focused educational experience that nurtures the mind, body, and spirit of every child. Situated in the vibrant heart of Purano Kalimati / Kalanki area, Kathmandu, our modern campus is designed to be a safe, welcoming haven for your child's exploration and growth.

Our dedicated faculty understands our local culture and blends it with global standards, ensuring that students from Playgroup to Grade 5 receive the highest quality education tailored to their unique needs and aspirations.

Modern Infrastructure

Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms and safe play areas designed for active exploration.

Experienced Faculty

Qualified educators dedicated to nurturing each child's unique potential and local values.

Holistic Development

Balanced growth across academics, sports, arts, and character building.

Safe Environment

Secure campus with CCTV surveillance and a protected atmosphere for every child.
